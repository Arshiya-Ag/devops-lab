Work With Us

Build Your Career in Technology

Nova Systems is a team of dedicated technology professionals working together to deliver reliable IT solutions. We value technical expertise, continuous learning, and a collaborative approach to problem-solving.

Our Working Culture

Nova Systems fosters a collaborative, technically rigorous environment where professionals can grow their skills and contribute to meaningful client projects.

Technical Excellence

We maintain high technical standards across all our work, encouraging best practices in infrastructure, development, and security.

Continuous Learning

Technology evolves quickly. We support our team members in keeping their skills current through ongoing learning and practical experience.

Collaborative Teams

Our divisions work closely together, sharing knowledge and expertise across infrastructure, software, security, and support.

Client-Centred Work

Every project connects to a real business outcome. Our team develops a strong understanding of client needs alongside technical skills.

Knowledge Sharing

We encourage documentation, internal knowledge sharing, and mentorship to support team members at every stage of their career.

Professional Growth

Nova Systems is a place where technical professionals can take on increasing responsibility as they demonstrate expertise and reliability.

Our Technical Departments

At Nova Systems, our work is organised across four technical departments aligned to our service divisions. Each department brings specialist expertise while collaborating closely with the others.

Technical Operations

Infrastructure & Operations

Our infrastructure team manages Linux and Windows servers, networks, virtualisation environments, and monitoring systems for our clients. Work in this department involves hands-on systems administration and infrastructure design.

Relevant skills:

  • Linux
  • Windows Server
  • Networking
  • Virtualisation
  • Monitoring
Engineering

Software Development

The development team builds custom web applications, business management systems, APIs, and internal portals. We use modern web technologies and follow clean, maintainable coding practices.

Relevant skills:

  • Web Development
  • APIs
  • Databases
  • Software Architecture
Security

Security & Consulting

Our security consultants conduct infrastructure assessments, server hardening reviews, network security analysis, and IT audits. This team combines technical depth with strong advisory skills.

Relevant skills:

  • Security Assessments
  • Hardening
  • Compliance
  • Network Security
Support

Technical Support & Operations

The support team delivers helpdesk services, troubleshooting, and managed IT operations. This department is central to our client relationships and service continuity.

Relevant skills:

  • IT Support
  • Troubleshooting
  • Documentation
  • Service Management
