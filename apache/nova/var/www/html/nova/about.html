About Nova Systems

Built on Reliability.
Driven by Technology.

Nova Systems is an established B2B IT and software company dedicated to helping small and medium-sized businesses harness technology with confidence. From infrastructure to custom software, we deliver dependable solutions that keep operations running smoothly.

Our Company

Who We Are

Nova Systems is a medium-sized technology company built around a simple belief: businesses should be able to rely on their technology without it becoming a distraction. We serve small and medium-sized businesses across a broad range of sectors, acting as their dedicated IT and software partner rather than a transactional vendor.

Our team of around 50 professionals is organised into four specialist divisions — IT Infrastructure, Software Development, Security & Consulting, and Managed Services — giving us the depth to handle complex, multi-faceted technology challenges while remaining agile enough to respond quickly to individual client needs.

We focus exclusively on the B2B market, which means every process, tool, and habit we have developed is tuned to the realities of running business-critical systems — where reliability, security, and clear communication are non-negotiable.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Reliability

We build systems and form partnerships our clients can count on — day in, day out. Consistency is not a feature, it is our baseline.

Security

Protecting client data and business continuity is paramount. Security considerations are built into everything we design, deploy, and maintain.

Innovation

We stay ahead of technology trends so our clients don't have to, bringing relevant advances to business problems in practical and meaningful ways.

Transparency

Clear, honest communication at every stage — in pricing, project status, and risk — so our clients can make informed decisions with confidence.

Customer Satisfaction

Success is measured by the outcomes our clients achieve. We work collaboratively, listen carefully, and adapt to what matters most to each business.

Continuous Improvement

We regularly review our practices, tools, and processes — improving not just what we deliver, but how we deliver it, every engagement.

How We Think

Our Philosophy

Practical Over Complex

We prefer straightforward, maintainable solutions over unnecessary technical complexity. If a simpler approach delivers the same result with greater reliability, that is the right approach.

Long-Term Partnership

We aim to be a long-term technology partner, not a one-off vendor. The relationships we build are designed to grow alongside our clients as their business evolves.

Security is integrated into every system we build and manage — not bolted on afterwards. Protecting client data and business continuity starts at the design stage.
